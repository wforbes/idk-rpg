Where does the code editor live during a fight?

The classic four-corner layout works for picking a menu option. The question is what happens when you pick Attack or Spell: a code editor and a spellbook need far more room than a menu corner.

After picking Spell: three ways to make room

A

The bottom half becomes the editor

The battle scene shrinks to a strip at the top, and the editor and spellbook take the bottom half. This is closest to the old text-box feel and works on a wide screen or a tall phone.
